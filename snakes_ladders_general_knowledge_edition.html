How Rules Work: Every square (1–100) has a dedicated question. When you land on a Ladder Bottom or Snake Head, you answer that square's question first, and then move up the ladder or down the snake regardless of whether your answer was right or wrong (no second question is asked on the destination square). Knowledge points (+10 / +20 / +30, 0 for wrong) measure your intellect separately from dice luck!
